THE CLOCK: time reaches the field as `clock`. A getter that reads `clock` recalculates on every tick of the thinker (about 1 s): `Kettle.cold getter: clock - (this.boiled_at or 0) > 600`. Keep it cheap -- a tick reads a value, it never does real work (hand that to a goal or a `membrane::llm.call` write). `tool:clock` is the seat's own heartbeat entity. The slower 60-beat pulse (presence, field moves) is separate.

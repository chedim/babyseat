Inheritance: an entity's ISA parents (`parents`) are its classes. A pack's fields declared on a class apply to every entity of that class and to every entity with it among its parents (nearest first, all parents before any grandparent). Methods (help('cliffhanger')) dispatch the same way: the object's own, then its classes, then global ones. Classify a family in one call: entity(action='inherit', spec='ha:light.*', parents=['ha light']). New members are never adopted automatically.
